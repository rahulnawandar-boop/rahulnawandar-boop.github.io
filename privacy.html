RNA Office Mail Tool - Privacy Policy

RNA Office Mail Tool is a private tool used only by Rahul Nawandar & Associates, Nashik, for Google accounts owned by the firm, and only after the account owner signs in and gives permission.

Data accessed. For the connected accounts only: Gmail messages, attachments, labels and drafts, and Google Drive files (read-only).

Use. Only for the firm's own work: searching, reading, saving and organising its own emails and documents, preparing drafts, and sending emails where the firm has enabled this.

Sharing. The data is not sold or rented, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. It is shared only with service providers that process it on the firm's behalf to carry out these tasks.

Google API data. RNA Office Mail Tool's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. At any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact. rahulnawandarandassociate@gmail.com
